## Claim disposition

The README preserves previous numbers and figures for comparison, but its warning applies to
**every old analytical result**, including stated-versus-filed, outlier, trend, election,
outcome, reuse and roll-call claims. They are unresolved until rebuilt and reviewed.
The old 62% top-1 / 78% top-2 figures do not validate the replacement or establish reliable
topic-specific performance. Source subject-tag agreement is not automatically accuracy,
independent validation, or an upper bound.

The existing 50-plank snapshot is a known, previously inspected diagnostic set. It is not
training data or a fresh blind test. New assistant/local-model judgments, if collected, must
retain that reviewer type and must not be described as human gold.

## Legislative extraction contract

The extractor now stages all eleven bill, sponsorship, action, vote and richer-evidence
tables before promotion. Its source-hashed manifest starts incomplete, existing artifacts
cannot be overwritten, and successful extraction is explicitly **not source completeness
or scientific certification**. An interrupted run retains its staging directory and the
original dump; select a new output directory rather than overwriting its evidence.

PostgreSQL COPY parsing now fails on malformed rows, missing source-table blocks and
unterminated blocks instead of silently dropping evidence. Control escapes and encoded
UTF-8 are preserved. Abstracts, source URLs, versions and version links retain original IDs,
notes and dates with exact bill joins and per-bill availability counts. Empty URL fields
do not count as available references. Snapshot abstracts and signed-act references are
**not automatically filing-time-safe**, and version text has not yet been downloaded.

The extraction manifest intentionally leaves the analytical cutoff unset. Retaining source
context from sessions overlapping 2018 does not mean every retained row belongs in a
2018-onward analytical denominator. Actual dates, missingness and session completeness must
be measured after extraction.

## Measured source limitations after extraction

Of 1,090,369 retained source-context records, 7,594 precede 2018 and must not enter an
unqualified 2018-onward denominator. There are 24,661 missing first-action dates and
14 latest-action dates after the October 18 observation date. Future dates may represent
scheduled effectiveness rather than corrupt data; they require source/action review.

The snapshot supplies nonempty abstracts for 376,070 measures and version references for
1,069,117. The retained sidecars contain 388,389 abstracts, 1,743,057 source URLs,
1,899,909 version records and 2,617,690 version links. Reference availability is not full-text
retrieval or proof that a summary describes the introduced version.

**Historical party attribution is a major unresolved source gap, not a reason to restore the
old current-party fallback.** The dump has 21,993 party memberships, of which 21,810
have no date bounds. Under the strict dated-membership rule, 1,081,990 measures remain
unknown; only 39 D, 21 R and two other-party measures resolve, while Nebraska's 8,317
remain nonpartisan. These outputs cannot support D/R agenda comparisons.

A second source was recovered rather than assuming the dump was the only option:
the CC0 Open States people repository at revision
`bf4caf105dc0e4fbd52d06e970ec264540f5da97` (September 24, 2026).
Its archive SHA-256 is
`f0e96e3c5737d25852c7d7b242dce71f34f8a3b7c8dc271dc4fc464f6774b5cf`.
The inspected state legislature/retired files contain 21,886 people; 20,760 party records
are undated, and only 183 have any date bound. This source therefore does not by itself
repair the temporal gap. Legislative tenure and file creation/update dates are not silently
substituted for party-affiliation dates.

The audit checks completed-extraction hashes before reading data, requires exact bill/evidence

## Provisional supervised-label preparation

The three retained CAP files contain 508,287 rows. They are not interchangeable with the
adapted project taxonomy: reproductive-health, tax, election-access/administration and
family/culture boundaries require review. Original and harmonized code columns can differ;
inconsistent major/subtopic combinations also exist. The initialization preparer verifies
the exact source hashes, quarantines known incompatibilities, encoding damage and short
context-dependent units, excludes duplicate/close target text, and retains the original
codes and source-row references for selected records. It does not turn an unflagged row into
project gold.

The reviewed culture migration is CAP `23/2300` from legacy PAP `6/609`, not equality between
the two code columns. Current local preparation selects 5,895 external initialization units
across all 21 project topics, capped at 100 per source/topic and dated 2000 onward.
**Culture has only ten selected examples**, and these cover arts, not the adapted category's
full family/moral scope. This remains a serious training-support limitation.

The official CAP source-use statement was retained with provenance as
`raw/cap/source_use.html`. Project-generated variables carry CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 terms;
original-text rights can also apply. The platform codebook identifies its source units as
quasi-sentences, not necessarily standalone state-level policy commitments.
Source/model redistribution is not approved by this research preparation.

A 200-unit diagnostic packet contains two bills per state and 100 units from the newly
recovered official 2026 Texas Republican platform. The latter is one document, not a
representative state-platform sample. Fifty initial units (40 titles and ten platform units)
have explicit assistant-review identities, dates, rationales and exact evidence bindings,
including non-policy/mixed/insufficient-evidence abstentions. All are development diagnostics;
none are independent human gold or a blind certification test. The user was unavailable
to choose a reviewer arrangement; no human-review commitment is assumed.

The Texas PDF exposes 285 consecutively numbered policy planks. Paragraph-only extraction
had collapsed the document into 73 large units. Number-aware extraction now retains those
285 planks separately and preserves surrounding front matter and appendices: 299 total
source units, not 299 verified policy commitments. Source offsets remain exact, and
front matter is not silently dropped or labelled as substantive policy.

The dataset and review artifacts remain ignored under `data/labels/`. The preparer CLI is:


This prepares a research initialization corpus, not a certified replacement classifier.
Independent target review, a finalized rubric, broader state/party examples, family leakage
review, calibration and empirical release gates remain outstanding.
