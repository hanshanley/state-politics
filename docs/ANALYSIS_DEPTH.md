# Analytical depth and data requirements

This is a research-capability inventory, not certification of the current results.
`docs/AUDIT_STATUS.md` records recovered evidence, measured classifier failures and
the unresolved scientific/artifact gates. A machine-readable artifact inventory is
generated by:

```bash
uv run python -m state_politics.analysis.coverage
```

The machine-readable outputs are:

- `data/processed/analysis_capabilities.json`
- `data/processed/analysis_capabilities.csv`

## Source availability is not current analytical support

The capability report leaves current stated agendas, reliable within-party stated
comparisons and operative platform coverage unsupported. A heuristic-confirmed
2018+ document is a source candidate, not a verified operative platform. Undated
records are reported separately rather than called current. A legislative caucus
source does not certify a state party committee's agenda.

Organization counts with dated, undated and any-date source records can overlap.
They are availability descriptions, not adoption determinations or evidence that
the remaining organizations publish no platform. The analytical cutoff remains
unset and all 100 operative-platform statuses remain unresolved.

The old 30-classified-unit rule is retained only as a labelled legacy diagnostic.
Passing it does not establish accurate labels, adequate source coverage or reliable
within-party comparability. Both trained ModernBERT candidates remain provisional:
each makes 27/50 correct decisions on the same assistant-reviewed development set,
matching rather than exceeding the original baseline.

## Implemented research methods, not approved findings

The repository contains state-party atlas and peer comparisons, topic-profile
similarity, literal language concentration, election-title detectors and duplicate
clusters. It also implements equal-state temporal comparisons, paired sign-flip
tests and multiple-testing adjustments. These methods do not repair invalid
source selection, labels, historical party attribution or filing-time evidence.

Source subject tags can provide an additional comparison but are neither universal
nor an independent certification of all classifier outputs. A detected election
title is not, by itself, a verified substantive election-policy measure.

Retained actions, motion results and historical organization links permit further
outcome and roll-call research. Existing sample-size/action-coverage thresholds
do not establish party-at-filing eligibility or complete outcome semantics.
Most recovered party memberships lack date bounds; current-party backfilling
must not silently restore apparent coverage.

Other legacy capability-report support labels describe artifact/schema availability,
not scientific release approval. This source-reporting repair does not certify or
regenerate the remaining bill-topic, trend, outcome or voting analyses.

## Requirements before comparative claims

Fix the analytical cutoff and reconcile the full organization/source ledger.
Verify source adoption and operative versions, distinguish proposals and
ceremonies, preserve native policy boundaries and review uncertain extraction.
Resolve historical affiliation and filing-time text eligibility independently
of the topic model.

Finalize the substantive rubric and target support, establish an appropriate
reviewer arrangement and representative held-out evaluation, and predeclare
empirical release gates. Development-document exposure must remain disclosed.
Regenerate dependent analyses only after their input and scientific gates pass;
neither increasing document counts nor lowering training loss authorizes release.

## Causal and substantive limits

Even validated descriptive differences would not identify causal party effects.
Majority control, agenda access, bill mix, institutional rules and selection into
roll calls are not randomized. Votes concern particular motions; topic attention
does not identify support/opposition or a one-dimensional ideology.
